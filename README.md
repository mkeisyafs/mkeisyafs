### About Me

I am a full-stack engineer and the founder of **[Rabux (PT Rabux Digital Karsa)](https://rabux.id)**, a modern web development studio and AI solutions consultancy based in Bandung. 

My work centers around engineering high-performance digital platforms, building robust multi-tenant web systems, and integrating autonomous AI workflows into scalable web applications. I care deeply about clean software architecture, operational efficiency, and shipping practical business value over industry hype.

- 🏢 **Current Focus**: Scaling **[Rabux](https://rabux.id)** & building client automation engines
- 🛠️ **Core Technologies**: TypeScript, React, Node.js, Bun, Elysia, Docker, AWS
- ⚡ **Interests**: Full-stack architecture, Model Context Protocol (MCP), and agentic workflows
